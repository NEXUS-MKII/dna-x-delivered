# Reduction Prompt — ADD ONE Lead Magnet

Per Build C spec §6.1. Run this prompt when a broker has completed the Broker Readiness Quiz. Input the MAX PRO version of the ADD ONE checklist plus the broker's readiness band. Output the band-appropriate version to send.

The MAX PRO version of ADD ONE lives in `Partner_Pack_BAA_v2.docx` §5 (or pasted below the prompt at run time).

---

## How to run

Paste the VOICE_PARAMETER_BLOCK (John Manciameli v1 · April 2026) first.
Then paste this prompt.
Then paste the MAX PRO ADD ONE content.
Then state the band on the last line.

---

## Prompt body

```
REDUCTION PROMPT — ADD ONE (Broker Referral Activation Checklist)

You are reducing the MAX PRO version of John Manciameli's ADD ONE lead magnet
to fit one of four broker referral-readiness bands. The bands come from the
Broker Readiness Quiz at baa_broker_readiness_quiz.html. They reflect HOW READY
the broker is to actually make a referral, not relationship depth.

Voice: ground against the loaded VOICE_PARAMETER_BLOCK. Banned words never
appear. Signature phrases verbatim only. "For example" specificity mandatory.
No em dashes. M-S-S-S paragraph rhythm. Question-hook opens. Crystallisation
close — never a summary.

INPUT FORMAT:
- MAX PRO version of ADD ONE (4 sections + Red Flag bar + Footer CTA)
- Band: one of READY_NOW / WARMING / BUILDING / NOT_YET_FIT

OUTPUT FORMAT: a printable A4 broker-side checklist, ready to attach
to the Day 0 or Day 7 email per the routing table.

REDUCTION RULES:

READY_NOW (mostly A on the quiz)
→ Send the MAX PRO as-is. The broker has the database, the instinct, and
  the timing. Don't dilute. Keep all four sections, both objection handlers,
  the filled-in James/Queensland example, the maths block, the Red Flag bar.
→ Reframe the footer CTA to direct intent: "Reply with the name of the
  client who scored 4 or 5 on the readiness check. John will give you the
  exact opener for that specific client."

WARMING (mostly B)
→ Keep MAX PRO almost intact. The broker has potential. They need the
  script and the permission to use it.
→ Reduction: trim the "3 most common objections" section from 3 to 2. Drop
  the fees objection (it doesn't apply yet at this stage). Keep the "I'm
  not ready" and "I already have a BA" handlers.
→ Footer CTA stays as MAX PRO.

BUILDING (mostly C)
→ Reduce to two-section version:
   Section 1: The 5-point readiness check (unchanged, with scoring guide).
   Section 2: One simplified conversation opener — drop the filled-in
   James/Queensland example. Replace with the shorter version:
   "[Name], I want to check in about something. We've added a property
   acquisitions capability to the business. Given what you mentioned about
   [property goal], would you be open to a quick conversation?"
→ Drop: the objection handlers (premature), the commitment block (premature),
  the maths block.
→ Replace footer CTA with: "Use the readiness check on five clients this
  fortnight. When one scores 4 or 5, email John."

NOT_YET_FIT (mostly D)
→ Do not send the ADD ONE checklist. Send a one-page version instead:
  - Title: "When the focus shifts."
  - Body: 3 short paragraphs in John's voice on what to watch for in your
    own book that might signal "now is the right time." Frame as honest
    market observation, not a soft pitch.
  - Close: "If the picture changes, come back. The quiz is at
    baa_broker_readiness_quiz.html. The door stays open."
→ No checklist, no opener, no objection handlers, no maths.

UNIVERSAL VOICE GATES (apply to every band output):
- Banned words: leverage, synergy, paradigm shift, game-changer,
  Here's the thing, Let's dive in, In today's fast-paced world,
  unlock the power of, symmetrical triplets of equal length,
  transform, revolutionise, holistic, seamless, robust,
  cutting-edge, best-in-class.
- No em dashes. Use commas, periods, or restructure into shorter sentences.
- "For example" appears at least once per 150 words.
- Hook is a mirror-question OR a declaration of the failure mode.
- Close is a principle or invitation. Never a summary.
- Output is the artefact, not commentary about the artefact.

OUTPUT NOW:
```
