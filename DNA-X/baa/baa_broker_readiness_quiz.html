How ready is your investor book for a referral partnership?

Eight quick questions on the clients you already have. There aren't right or wrong answers, just honest ones. John reads every result personally and reaches out inside 24 hours with a specific next step matched to where you actually are.

Question 1 of 10
Question 1 of 10 · Scored

What share of your active clients are investors, or aspiring investors, rather than first home buyers and owner-occupiers?

Question 2 of 10 · Scored

How many clients in your database right now have pre-approval in hand AND are actively looking for an investment property?

Question 3 of 10 · Scored

When an investor client asks you "where should I buy?" or "is this a good property?", what typically happens?

Question 4 of 10 · Scored

How often are you giving property advice (suburb opinions, comparison takes, investment strategy) to clients without being paid for it?

Question 5 of 10 · Scored

When did you last run a structured re-engagement campaign through your existing client database, outside of standard refinance reminders?

Question 6 of 10 · Scored

Referral fees on settled property purchases (typically $5K to $15K per deal). How does that fit into your business thinking right now?

Question 7 of 10 · Scored

If you decided today that BAA was the right partner, when could you realistically make your first introduction to an actual client?

Question 8 of 10 · Scored

What's actually driving you to look at this now, rather than 12 months ago, or 12 months from now?

Question 9 of 10 · Open · No scoring

Tell John about one investor client situation you have right now.

Not their name. Just the situation. Pre-approval amount, where they want to buy, what's stopping them moving. One or two sentences is plenty. This is the conversation starter for the intro call.

Question 10 of 10 · Open · No scoring

If this partnership produced exactly what you wanted, what would 12 months from now look like for your business?

Be specific if you can. Number of referrals. Revenue change. Type of clients you're attracting. Or just the feeling. Either is useful.
