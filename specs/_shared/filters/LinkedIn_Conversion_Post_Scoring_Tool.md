LinkedIn Post Scoring Tool 
(Leads vs Likes) 
What this is!
A simple scoring tool to predict whether a LinkedIn post is likely to generate inbound leads vs just likes.
This is the scoring system we’ll use for RR26 speaker content support (Farhad + Tim and others):
Build a baseline draft that gets as close to 70/100 as possible
Then add speaker-specific elements (their lived examples, proof, vocabulary, polarity) to push it higher

Minimum ship score + gates
Minimum ship score: 70/100 (“First Draft” standard)
PASS (70+): publishable first draft
HOLD (60–69): needs targeted edits before publishing
KILL (<60): wrong audience / no gift / no step-through (rewrite from scratch)

Copy/paste prompt (use as-is)
I’m going to give you a LinkedIn post and a description of my business, who I sell to, and what my offer is. Tell me the percentage chance this post generates an inbound lead vs just gets likes.
Score it using the system below. Then tell me whether it is PASS (70+), HOLD (60-69), or KILL (<60).
Here’s how to score it:

Chris Coherence tests
Feed your main strategic outcomes (All campaigns) 

STEP 1 - THE WINDOW TEST (does it stop the right person?)
Check the Reflection: - Does the reader see THEMSELVES in the first 2 lines? Not “that’s interesting” - “that’s ME.” - Score the identity match: are there specific nouns from their world (their job, their tools, their daily frustrations) or is it generic? - Score: 0-20
Check the Gift: - Once they click “see more,” is there an obvious payoff? A framework, a diagnosis, a belief flip, a “finally someone gets it” moment? - Or is it vague inspiration? - Score: 0-20
Check the Step-Through: - Is there a clear next action? (DM, comment, profile visit, link click) - Or does the post just end? - Score: 0-10
Check the Present Test: - If they click through to my profile after reading this, will they find more content that matches? - Or will they think “wait, this wasn’t actually for me”? - (I’ll tell you about my profile/offer so you can judge this.) - Score: 0-10
STEP 2 - LANGUAGE MATCH (does it sound like their world?)
Check across 5 layers: - Functional: Does it use the VERBS of their daily life? (Not marketing-speak - their actual routines) - Technical: Does it use insider vocabulary only they’d know? - Cultural: Does it reference shared experiences from their world? - Psychic: Does it voice the internal monologue they never say out loud? - Narrative: Does it describe the repeating story loop of their life? - Score: 0-20

STEP 3 - CARRIER TRUST (do they trust the human behind this?)
Check these signals in the post: - Lived Credibility: Does the author sound like they’ve actually done this, not just studied it? - Proof Proximity: Are there results from people like the reader? - Motive Transparency: Is it clear why the author is sharing this? - Values Polarity: Does this post attract the RIGHT people and repel the wrong ones? Or does everyone “kind of agree”? - Score: 0-20
THEN GIVE ME: 1. Total score out of 100 2. PASS/HOLD/KILL rating 3. Translate that to: “X% chance this generates an inbound lead, Y% chance it just gets likes” 4. The #1 reason it will fail to convert (be specific and direct) 5. Rewrite my hook (first 2 lines) so the right person’s brain screams “that’s me” 6. One line I should add that would spike Carrier Trust
Be brutal. I don’t want encouragement. I want a diagnosis.
