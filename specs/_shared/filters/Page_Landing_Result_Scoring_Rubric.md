Page Scoring Rubric
Asset-mechanics diagnostic for landing pages (Sales / Partner / Lead Magnet Capture) and quiz result pages.

Source Material
This rubric is calibrated against the following — including external CRO research because no NOW-internal LP framework existed in project knowledge at build time:
Hormozi Value Equation — Dream Outcome × Perceived Likelihood / (Time × Effort)
CXL Heuristic Analysis (Peep Laja) — Clarity, Relevancy, Friction, Distraction
Attention Ratio principle (Oli Gardner / CXL) — 1:1 most-wanted action, exception only when multiple links target same goal
Message Match (CXL) — pre-click promise = post-click headline
Ufrenza dos/don’ts — pain first, benefits not features, action-verb CTAs, real urgency only
NOW DNA worked example (nowgroup.co.nz/now-dna) — risk reversal micro-copy, failure-mode naming, tier ladder, FAQ as objection handling
Quiz Build LVL One MVP — band-matched result page architecture (4 result pages)
Universal Quiz Funnel Builder — result page skeleton (Headline / What this means / Top 3 gaps / 3-step fix / 1 CTA)

Voice Layer Note
This is an asset-mechanics rubric. Voice integrity is NOT scored here — it is handled by pairing the Voice Parameter Block (Encoder Pro / Encoder Lite) at score time. Run voice gate separately. This rubric scores ONLY whether the asset works structurally as the asset type it claims to be.

What this is
A scoring tool to predict whether a page converts the right reader into the next step — or gets a polite scroll and a tab close.
Scope: standalone pages where the reader has arrived with intent. Covers both Build F landing pages and Build D quiz result pages. Use the subtype profile that matches the page you’re scoring.

Core Principle
A good page does three jobs:
Confirms the right person has landed — within 5 seconds, above the fold (60% never scroll past)
Delivers the promise without making them work for it (Hormozi Value Equation, CXL Clarity)
Lands on ONE earned next step (Attention Ratio 1:1)
A page is not a website. It is a conversion path with copy on it. Every line either earns the next line or it gets cut.

Gates
PASS (70+): publishable. Push live and watch behaviour.
HOLD (60–69): page works but conversion will be soft. Run upgrade pass.
KILL (<60): wrong audience, vague promise, attention ratio broken, or above-fold fails. Rewrite from headline.

Asset Standard (must contain)
Universal across all subtypes:
Above-the-fold: headline + sub-headline + CTA + 1 risk reversal micro-line — all visible without scrolling on mobile
Headline answers Who/What/WIIFM in 5 seconds
ONE most-wanted action (Attention Ratio 1:1) — multiple buttons OK only if all funnel to same goal
At least one form of proof (vignette, number, before/after, testimonial, logo)
At least one objection silently neutralised
No nav bar pulling away from conversion
Mobile-readable (no slabs, breathing room)
If the page has more than one most-wanted action, score caps at 65 (CXL Distraction principle).

Subtype Profiles
Pick the profile that matches the page being scored. Each profile shifts the scoring weights:

Profile A — Sales Page (Build F)
Reader state: cold or warm, evaluating purchase. Worked example: NOW DNA page.
Heaviest dimension: VALUE EQUATION — Dream Outcome + Risk Reversal carry the buy decision
Required: pricing visible, tier ladder if applicable, FAQ handling top 5 objections, vignettes/testimonials
Common failure: features-not-benefits, no risk reversal, FAQ that dodges real objections
Profile B — Partner Page (Build F)
Reader state: a referral partner deciding whether to advocate. Reader needs to confidently describe what you do, who you help, and how to refer.
Heaviest dimension: AUDIENCE LOCK — ICP description must be precise enough that they recognise their own clients
Required: ICP described in 3 specific client types, services with outcomes, clear referral process, brand assets, contact preferences
Common failure: vague ICP, "we help businesses grow" generality, no clear referral process
Profile C — Lead Magnet Capture Page (Build F)
Reader state: arrived from an ad, post, or DM keyword wanting the magnet. Bounce rate dependency on form friction.
Heaviest dimension: FRICTION — form length, field count, value-to-friction ratio
Required: specific magnet promise, what’s inside (3–5 bullets), short form (email + name max), instant delivery promise
Common failure: long form, vague magnet promise, hidden delivery delay
Profile D — Quiz Result Page (Build D)
Reader state: just completed the quiz, has score, expects personalised insight. Per Quiz Build LVL One MVP architecture.
Heaviest dimension: PERSONALISATION + SEQUEL PULL — they took action, page must reward it
Required: score visible at top, top 3 gaps tied to answers, 3-step fix, ONE band-matched CTA, calm operator tone (no shame, no doom)
Common failure: generic content not tied to answers, shame-based copy, mismatched CTA tier

Copy/Paste Prompt
I am going to give you a page (sales / partner / LM capture / quiz result) and a description of my business, audience, and the next step I want them to take. Score whether the right person who lands on this page actually takes the next step — or scrolls, hesitates, and closes the tab. Score using the rubric below. Return PASS (70+), HOLD (60-69), or KILL (<60).
Tell me which subtype profile to apply (Sales / Partner / LM Capture / Quiz Result). Apply that profile’s heaviest-dimension weighting to the score.

STEP 1 — ABOVE-THE-FOLD (35 pts) — heaviest weight (Hormozi: 60% never scroll past; CXL: top-priority within fold)
Headline mirror (Who/What/WIIFM in 5 seconds) (0–15):
Does the right reader see THEMSELVES in the headline?
Or does it describe the offer? ("We help businesses unlock...")
Specific nouns from their world (their role, their tools, their daily problem)?
Sub-headline names dream outcome (0–10):
Specific outcome in plain English?
Could the reader say "yes — that’s what I want" within 5 seconds?
Or vague/jargon-heavy ("transform your strategy", "unlock your potential")?
Above-fold CTA + risk reversal visible (0–10):
CTA visible without scrolling on mobile?
Risk reversal micro-line below CTA? ("FREE • 5 MINUTES • NO CARD REQUIRED" pattern)
Plain-English next-step button copy ("Send me the framework"), not meaningless ("Submit")?

STEP 2 — VALUE EQUATION (25 pts) — Hormozi
Dream outcome (0–8):
Articulated specifically — what they actually want?
Status / time / money / outcome named, not implied?
Perceived likelihood (proof) (0–7):
At least one vignette, number, or before/after?
Trust signals — testimonials, case studies, logos?
Or unsupported claims?
Time signal (0–5):
How fast they get the result — quantified or implied? ("by the end of the week", "ships in 24 hours")
Or vague? ("get results faster")
Effort / sacrifice reduction (0–5):
Done-for-you, easy setup, white glove, 3 minutes — what makes it low-effort?
Or implies hard work the reader has to do?

STEP 3 — ATTENTION RATIO + RELEVANCY (20 pts) — CXL Distraction + Relevancy
Attention ratio 1:1 (0–10):
ONE most-wanted action? (Or all CTAs funnel to same goal)
No nav bar / footer / sidebar pulling attention away?
No competing CTAs in the body?
Message match (0–5):
Headline matches the source (ad / link / button / DM keyword) that brought them?
Pre-click and post-click promise consistent?
Audience lock (0–5):
Right reader recognises themselves in the body within first 30 seconds of reading?
Specific role / context language vs. "for businesses"?

STEP 4 — FRICTION + POST-CONVERSION (20 pts) — CXL Friction + Ufrenza
Friction audit (0–10):
Form short and only asks what’s necessary?
Mobile-readable — no slabs, breathing room?
Page load expectation reasonable (no heavy assets above fold)?
Objection handling (0–5):
At least one major objection silently neutralised in body or FAQ?
Or assumes reader is already convinced?
Post-conversion clarity (0–5):
What happens after they convert? (Thank you message, instant delivery, next-step instructions?)
Or do they vanish into the void?

Upgrade Pass (HOLD → PASS)
Headline (+5 to +10): Replace offer-speak with reader-speak. Use a noun from their world. Test: would the right person say "yes that’s me" within 2 seconds at 7am on a phone?
Sub-headline (+3 to +5): Name the specific outcome in plain English. Cut every adjective that doesn’t pull weight.
Risk reversal (+3 to +5): Add a micro-line under the CTA following the NOW DNA pattern: "FREE • [TIME] • [LOW-FRICTION SIGNAL]". Examples: "FREE DIAGNOSTIC • 5 MINUTES • NO CARD REQUIRED" / "INSTANT DELIVERY • NO COMMITMENT" / "5 QUESTIONS • 3 MINUTES • PERSONALISED RESULT".
Proof (+5 to +10): Add one vignette, one number, or one before/after. Don’t add three. Add one. Make it specific.
CTA (+3 to +5): If two CTAs compete, kill one. Replace meaningless button copy with plain-English next step.
Time + effort signal (+3 to +5): Add a quantified time-to-result and an effort-reduction line.

Common Failure Modes
Headline describes the offer, not the reader’s world
Sub-headline is jargon-heavy ("unlock", "transform", "strategic")
Above-fold doesn’t pass 5-second test
Two CTAs competing — "book a call OR download the guide"
Button copy is meaningless ("Submit", "Click here", "Get started")
Body is feature-and-benefit list, no vignette, no proof
No risk reversal micro-line
No time signal — reader doesn’t know how fast
Page assumes the reader is already convinced — no objection handling
Mobile slab — paragraph longer than 4 lines on a phone
Nav bar pulling attention to other site pages
Fake urgency / fake scarcity
Stock photo handshake (CXL fail pattern)
No post-conversion clarity
